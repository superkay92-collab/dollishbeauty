Booking & preparation

  • Bookings are recommended 1–3 months before the wedding and are subject to availability. Verbal agreements do not secure a date.
  • Additional services requested after confirmation will attract additional charges.
  • Share makeup inspiration, allergies, sensitivities and skin concerns before the appointment. Wedding-day look changes may attract an extra charge.
  • The bride provides frontal units or hair extensions and must make them available within the agreed preparation timeframe.
  • Trials are optional and charged separately. Changes to the agreed look should be communicated before the wedding.
  • Bridal party services require advance confirmation and are priced separately.

Arrival, travel & cancellations

  • The bride and bridal party should be ready at the agreed appointment time. Delays, waiting time and extended hours may attract extra charges.
  • Studio and mobile services are available subject to confirmation. Transportation and accommodation expenses are charged separately.
  • Outside locations and very early call times must be discussed in advance.
  • Standard packages cover only the agreed service. Wedding-day touch-ups are not included; full-day attendance is available at an additional cost.
  • Deposits are non-refundable. Rescheduling is subject to availability and should be communicated as early as possible.
  • Please provide a clean, comfortable and adequately lit preparation area.

Dollish Beauty may request permission to photograph or record the completed look for portfolio and promotional use. Client privacy preferences will be respected. By paying the deposit and confirming a booking, you acknowledge that you have read and agreed to these policies.
