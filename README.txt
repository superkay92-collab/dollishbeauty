DOLLISH BEAUTY WEBSITE PREVIEW

FILES
- index.html: Website content and sections
- styles.css: Responsive minimal-luxury styling
- script.js: Mobile navigation, service selection, product enquiries and booking enquiry form

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP folder.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser. An internet connection is needed for Google Fonts and remote placeholder photography.

BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Replace the sample/stock photographs with your own Dollish Beauty work and actual hair product photos. Ensure you have permission to use all photos.
2. Confirm current service prices and add product prices, stock details, product specifications and delivery/returns policies.
3. The appointment form currently creates a WhatsApp enquiry; it does not check calendar availability or automatically confirm appointments. For real online scheduling, connect Wix Bookings or another scheduling service.
4. Product enquiry buttons currently open WhatsApp. This is a storefront design preview, not a live shopping cart or payment checkout. Connect a supported e-commerce and payment provider (for example, verify Wix + Paystack availability and compatibility in Ghana) before accepting online payments.
5. Confirm that the WhatsApp number and social links are correct.
6. Add your business address or studio-location instructions, privacy policy, terms, booking policy, cancellation policy and mobile travel fees.
7. To publish, upload the files to a static host or rebuild the design in Wix. The site is not live on a public domain yet.

CONTENT MANAGER (CMS-STYLE EDITOR)
- Open admin.html to edit the homepage copy, brand links, service cards, portfolio images, and hair products.
- Upload JPG/PNG images; the editor resizes them in your browser.
- Save on this device stores a preview copy in this browser only.
- Download website content file exports an updated cms-data.js. Replace the existing cms-data.js in your hosted website folder with the downloaded file, then upload/redeploy the updated website files.
- Export backup (JSON) creates a content backup you can import later.
- This is a static-site content editor, not a hosted multi-user database. It does not automatically sync edits across devices. For live editing from anywhere without redeploying, use a hosted CMS such as Wix CMS or connect a server-backed CMS.
- Product cards are enquiries, not a live checkout. Connect an e-commerce/payment provider before taking payments online.
